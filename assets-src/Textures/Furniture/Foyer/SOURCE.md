# L0 foyer furniture base colour (HOUSE-01038)

These 2048² sRGB RGBA PNGs are lossless PNG conversions of the base-colour JPEGs bundled with the two CC0 Poly Haven glTF models documented in `assets-src/Models/Furniture/Foyer/SOURCE.md`. They serve the canonical house static-material/chunk path; the GLBs retain the source's embedded base-colour, normal and ORM maps for model import. Generated deterministically by `tools/assets/foyer_furniture_prepare.py` and individually hashed in `assets-src/assets.manifest.json`.
