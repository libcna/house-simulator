# Garden furniture source

All six models in this directory are original project-authored geometry generated deterministically
by `tools/blender/garden_furniture_suite.py` and verified byte-for-byte by
`tools/assets/garden_furniture_prepare.py`. No third-party mesh, image or texture was copied.

The models are distributed under the repository's `LICENSE` (Ms-PL). Their exact hashes, measured
bounds, triangle counts, collision proxies, material mappings and exterior users are recorded in
`assets-src/assets.manifest.json`.
