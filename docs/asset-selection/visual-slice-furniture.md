The five heavier objects have LOD1/LOD2 node meshes generated by `lod_gen.py`; largest-component silhouette errors were at most 0.0014 of the measured bound. Collision proxies enclose the full LOD0 object (not just its biggest source material mesh), stay below 64 triangles, and are excluded from rendering. The original material slots are explicitly mapped to canonical sRGB furniture albedos by `materialMap`; generic repainting would lose cushion, shade, leaf and rug detail. The six NPOT albedos are resized to power-of-two dimensions because the first actual Reach-profile capture failed on the source's 800² wrapped cushion image; normalized UV-to-colour mapping is retained.

The imported source GLBs retain nearest/linear sampling but no longer request unavailable embedded
mipmaps. The TV/media-unit slots that declared physical transmission use their textured opaque
base-colour fallback in this strict XNA static route; treating transmission factor 1 as CNA alpha
would make those parts almost disappear. This is a consciously limited screen/cabinet finish, not
a claim that the house has physically rendered decorative glass.

Deferred: a second sofa design and full decor density. A repeated copy in each of two different rooms is acceptable for the first playable slice, but further rooms should vary the seating language.

The 46,492-triangle source stores duplicated disconnected triangle corners. Direct decimation tore

which retains the soft trim silhouette without glow or the first capture's pale stipple. The
existing `PROP_LIVING_SOFA` id, measured placement and collision semantics remain unchanged.
