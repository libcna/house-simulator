# First foyer furnishing selection (HOUSE-01038)

The vertically connected entrance needed actual scaled furniture, not procedural boxes. Official Poly Haven model previews were checked before acquisition, then the 2K geometry/maps and built GLBs were validated through the CNA importer. Both sources are CC0-1.0 and have manifest provenance; no licence expansion was needed.

| Asset | Why selected | Measured cost/scale | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Chinese Console Table](https://polyhaven.com/a/chinese_console_table) | Narrow, dark carved timber reads as an entry console, stylistically fitting the Colonial shell | Source 1.720 × 0.661 × 0.339 m, 13,648 visible triangles, 2K atlas, 24-triangle `_COL`; placed at 92 % = 1.582 m wide | A full 1.72 m entry console cannot occupy the foyer's short side-wall strips because the living/stair portals interrupt them. The carefully scaled south-east position under the sidelight leaves the front-door swing and casing clear. |
| [Arm Chair 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/ArmChair_01) | Pale upholstered seat and warm dark carved wood break up the stone/ivory room | 0.848 × 1.065 × 0.766 m, seat baseline ~0.47 m, 5,626 visible triangles, 2K atlas, 48-triangle `_COL` | The pinned support axis was 3.4 cm off-centre in Z; its actual POSITION stream is recentered before `_COL` generation, preserving scale and passing the 2 cm origin gate. |

The console occupies X 0.600…2.182, Z −14.676…−14.364 m; it begins 0.100 m outside the front-door aperture and 0.010 m outside the frame casing. After the centre correction, the chair occupies X 1.217…1.983, Z −18.124…−17.276 m, at least 0.176 m from the stair and hall portal planes. The hall opening retains 2.752 m unobstructed width and the central entrance route substantially more than the required 1.2 m. Actual visual review and door/collision tests are recorded in `docs/visual-review/README.md`.
