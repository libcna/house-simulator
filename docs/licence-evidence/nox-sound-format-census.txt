NOX Sound "Essentials Series" — measured audio format census
============================================================

HOUSE-00276. Produced with ffprobe over every .wav in the collection at
/rv/tmp/Essentials_Series_NOX_SOUND/ on 2026-09-07.

Regenerate with:
    find <dir> -type f -iname '*.wav' \
      | while IFS= read -r f; do ffprobe -v error -select_streams a:0 \
          -show_entries stream=codec_name,sample_rate,channels,bits_per_raw_sample \
          -of csv=p=0 "$f"; done | sort | uniq -c | sort -rn

This is a SUMMARY plus every outlier, not 1 644 lines: the per-file listing is
regenerable in a minute and would be a large committed file nobody reads. What
is not regenerable once the /rv/tmp/ copy is gone is the fact that it WAS
measured, and what was found.

WHY IT MATTERS. The publisher's page states "48kHz / 24-bit" throughout (with
Sao Miguel at 96 kHz). That is true of the bulk and FALSE for 23 files. So
convert_audio.py (HOUSE-00193) must probe each input; it cannot assume the
stated format. Phase 1 already established that 48 kHz must not be blindly
resampled to 44.1 kHz -- this census is the evidence for how much of the
collection that decision governs: 1 624 of 1 644 files.
