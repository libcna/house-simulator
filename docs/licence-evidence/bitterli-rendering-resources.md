# Bitterli room-scene furniture — licence evidence

Retrieved 2026-09-15. The upstream [Bitterli scene README](https://github.com/gkjohnson/3d-demo-data/blob/main/models/bitterli-rendering-resources/README.md) attributes **The White Room** to Jay-Artist and **The Grey & White Room** to Wig42; it lists both as **CC BY 3.0**. The [Creative Commons legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode) permits copying, adaptation and commercial distribution with attribution. The full licence text is archived in `licenses/cc-by-3.0/`.

The selected per-object GLBs and their embedded base-colour images were extracted from those two scene files in `libcna/living-room-simulator`. That sibling's `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` records the same scene/author/licence chain. This repository rechecks source material slots, bounds and hashes, prepares LOD/collision meshes where appropriate, and gives each shipped source file its own manifest row. The attribution is generated into `licenses/THIRD-PARTY-ASSETS.md`.

Verdict: source and adapted geometry/textures may be redistributed by cna-house under CC BY 3.0 with the per-asset manifest attribution retained. These are **not** CC0 or project-owned assets.
